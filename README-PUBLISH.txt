IMMEVIA — PRODUCTION WEBSITE

Recommended static deployment path:
1. Create a Netlify site and deploy the contents of this folder.
2. The demo form is configured for Netlify Forms.
3. In Netlify, add the custom domain: immevia.ca
4. Netlify will show the DNS records to enter in GoDaddy.
5. In GoDaddy > Domains > immevia.ca > DNS, replace only the web-hosting records requested by Netlify.
   Do not change MX records if/when email is configured.
6. Add www.immevia.ca as an alias and redirect it to immevia.ca.
7. Enable HTTPS.
8. Test the form and verify submissions arrive in the site dashboard.
9. Before commercial launch, review the privacy/terms pages and replace their launch notes with final legal wording.

IMPORTANT:
- Do not buy GoDaddy Airo Website Builder for this site.
- The Loi 16 government link is external and already opens in a new tab.
- The dashboard content is illustrative and the site says so.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Applies to the site immevia.ca and the application app.immevia.ca. The French version prevails in case of difference.

Immevia is a building-management platform operated by Andrew Moubarak, sole proprietor doing business as Immevia, in Montréal (Québec). This policy explains, in plain language, what personal information we collect, why, where it is hosted, who we share it with and what your rights are. It is written to comply with Québec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (as amended by Law 25).

1. Person in charge of the protection of personal information

Andrew Moubarak, person in charge of the protection of personal information, Immevia, Montréal (Québec).
E-mail: mrandrewmoubarak@gmail.com · Phone: 514-265-0555. We answer requests within 30 days.

2. Two roles: your syndicate and Immevia

In the application, information about co-owners, tenants and suppliers is entered by the building’s syndicate or manager, which remains responsible for it towards residents. Immevia acts as a service provider: we host and process this information on the building’s behalf, following its instructions, and we do not use it for any other purpose. If you are a resident with a question about your information, your first contact is your syndicate or manager; we help them answer you.

3. What we collect

On the immevia.ca site

  • Free trial request form: name, e-mail, type of organisation, number of units, message. Nothing else is collected on the site; there is no audience analytics or advertising tool.

In the app.immevia.ca application

  • Account: name, e-mail, phone (optional), language, unit or role in the building, and the password (stored with irreversible hashing) or your Google identity if you choose that sign-in method.
  • Building finances: condo fees or rents billed, payments received, balances, reminders sent, payment plans. When a building turns on Interac transfer recognition, the transfer notice e-mails received at the building’s address are read to extract the sender’s name, the amount and the date; only these and a short excerpt of the e-mail (for the syndicate to check) are kept; no account number is recorded.
  • Maintenance: requests, photos, messages, availability, and for contractors engaged: name, company, phone, e-mail, quotes, invoices.
  • Documents and proof: documents uploaded by the syndicate (insurance policies, minutes, invoices, contracts, etc.) and the dates or amounts extracted from them.
  • Meetings: notices, replies, proxies, attendance, votes by unit, minutes.
  • Activity log: who did what and when in the application (for example, a payment recorded or a request closed). This log provides the traceability building management requires.
  • Subscription billing: name and e-mail of the person who pays, building and plan chosen, payment history and receipts. The card number is entered directly with our payment provider (Stripe); we only see the brand, the last 4 digits and the expiry date.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and sign-in timestamps, kept in security logs.

4. Why we use it

  • To provide the service: keep the building’s record, calculate balances, send notices, meeting notices, reminders and sign-in links.
  • To secure accounts and prevent unauthorised access.
  • To answer your requests and tell you about significant changes to the service.
  • To improve Immevia using aggregated or de-identified data (never identifiable records).

We do not sell personal information, we do no advertising profiling and we pass nothing to third parties for their own purposes.

5. Where your data is hosted

The building’s database and documents are hosted in Canada (Montréal region, with Supabase). Some processing does take place outside Québec, mainly in the United States: the servers that run the application, e-mail sending, automatic document recognition and card payments. When personal information leaves Québec, it goes through providers that offer equivalent contractual protection, and we have assessed the risks as the law requires. We only send outside what the processing in question needs.

7. How long

  • Building record (finances, meetings, maintenance, documents): as long as the building is a client, then according to its instructions. These records have legal value for the building (register, minutes, maintenance log); when the service ends, the building can export its data, and we delete it within 90 days of the end of the contract, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Resident accounts: deactivated by the syndicate when the resident leaves; past entries remain in the building’s register, without the e-mail or phone beyond what the register needs.
  • Subscription billing: receipts and payment history are kept for 6 years, as tax rules require.
  • Trial requests: 24 months.
  • Technical and security logs: 12 months at most.

8. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS) everywhere; hashed passwords; access to documents through time-limited signed links; each syndicate sees only its own building or buildings; an activity log; daily database backups; access to the service limited to the team that needs it. No system is infallible: in case of a confidentiality incident presenting a risk of serious harm, we will notify the people concerned and the Commission d’accès à l’information, as the law requires.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, withdraw your consent where processing relies on it, ask that the dissemination of information be stopped, and get a copy of the information you gave us in a structured format. Write to mrandrewmoubarak@gmail.com; we may ask you to confirm your identity. If you are a resident, we will handle the request with your syndicate or manager, which remains responsible for the building’s record. You can also file a complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

10. Cookies

The immevia.ca site uses no cookies. The application only uses necessary cookies: your sign-in session and the active building. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

11. Minors

Immevia is meant for syndicates, managers, owners and adult residents. We do not knowingly collect information about people under 14.

12. Changes

We update this policy when the service changes. The date at the top shows the latest version; significant changes are announced in the application or by e-mail to client syndicates.

1. The service

Immevia brings a building’s maintenance, finances, documents, meetings and communications together in one record. The service is provided as is, French first, and evolves regularly. The features available are those described in the application when you use it.

  • You are responsible for what is done with your credentials and personal sign-in links. Do not share them; tell us about any suspicious access.
  • Links sent to contractors give access to a single work request, without an account; the client can revoke them at any time.

7. Subscription, trial and payment

Price. The subscription is billed per building: $49 per month for a building of 10 units or fewer, plus $3.50 per month for each unit above 10. Residents, board members and contractors are included without limit. Paying yearly gives two months free (you pay 10 months out of 12). Prices are in Canadian dollars. No tax is added for now; if GST and QST become applicable, they will be added after at least 30 days’ notice.

Founder rate. The first 10 subscribed buildings get 25% off the current price for as long as their subscription stays active without interruption.

Free trial. Each building gets a 30-day free trial, with no credit card, starting when we send you your sign-in link. Nothing is charged during the trial, or after it, until you choose a subscription. If the trial ends without a subscription, your building stays viewable and exportable for 90 days, after which its data is deleted.

Payment. Payment is by card, through our payment provider Stripe; we never see or keep your card number. The subscription renews automatically every month or every year, as you choose, until you end it. A receipt is e-mailed to you for each payment.

Number of units. The price follows the building’s number of units in Immevia. A change applies from the next renewal.

Price changes. We e-mail you at least 30 days before any increase; it applies from the renewal after the notice, and you can end the subscription before it takes effect.

Cancellation and refunds. You can end your subscription at any time, from the application or by writing to us. Monthly subscription: it ends at the end of the month already paid, with no further charge; the current month is not refunded. Yearly subscription: we refund the full unused months.

Failed payment. If a payment fails, Stripe retries automatically and we let you know. After 30 days without payment, the building becomes read-only (viewing and export); after 60 days, we may end the service under section 9.
